# Mandate Charter

**The non-negotiable principles that define this project.**

This document exists so that no matter who works on Mandate in the future, and no matter how exciting or pressured the situation becomes, certain lines are never crossed.

---

## 1. We Are Building an Instrument, Not a Government

Mandate is a research and education platform. It is designed to help people understand legislation through the lens of their own values and to explore what large-scale personalized representation *could* look like.

**We will never**:
- Claim or imply that Mandate outputs have legal, electoral, or official force
- Present aggregate results as "the will of the people" or "the real mandate"
- Allow media, politicians, or anyone else to use Mandate data as authoritative public opinion without extremely prominent, repeated disclaimers
- Optimize for political influence or virality at the expense of accuracy and intellectual honesty

If someone tries to turn Mandate into a political weapon or legitimacy-laundering device, we will actively push back and clarify the limits.

---

## 2. The Human Is Always Sovereign Over Their Twin

Every person who uses Mandate completely owns their digital twin.

**This means**:
- Users can inspect, export, modify, copy, delete, or run their twin profile and model adapters anywhere, at any time, with no permission required from us
- Corrections and feedback from the user are the ultimate source of truth — not the model's prior
- We will never sell, share, or use personal preference data for any purpose the user did not explicitly and granularly consent to
- Users must be able to run a fully functional version of their twin locally (Ollama, local vector stores, exported adapters) without needing our servers

The goal is user emancipation, not platform lock-in.

---

## 3. Transparency Is Non-Negotiable

- All core code is open source (to be licensed under a strong reciprocal license)
- Model behavior, training data characteristics (where applicable), and limitations must be explainable to the user in plain language
- Every prediction must come with a traceable reasoning path back to the user's stated values or corrections
- We will publish our failures, biases we discover, and accuracy numbers — including when the system performs poorly for certain groups
- No secret sauce. No proprietary black boxes in the core experience.

---

## 4. We Start Narrow and Earn the Right to Grow

The 2018 vision is world-changing in scope. That is exactly why we will begin extremely small:

- United States federal legislation only in the first versions
- Individual use and small trusted cohorts only (no mass "public opinion" dashboards until the methodology is battle-tested)
- Strictly non-binding, experimental, and labeled as such everywhere
- No promises about future political adoption

We will measure success by whether real humans find the tool genuinely useful for understanding bills they would otherwise ignore — not by media coverage or political uptake.

---

## 5. We Actively Resist Capture

The problem we are trying to solve is the capture of representative systems by money and concentrated interests. We will not recreate that problem in new form.

**Therefore**:
- We will support multiple models and allow users to choose or bring their own
- We will prioritize decentralized and local-first architectures over convenient centralized ones as the project matures
- We will not accept funding that compromises the above principles (e.g., terms that would let a funder influence model behavior or data access)
- Governance of the project itself must eventually be distributed and protected against single-point control

---

## 6. We Prioritize Understanding Over Prediction

The highest value Mandate can deliver is not "your twin voted yes for you" but "here is what this 200-page bill actually does, broken down in a way that matters to someone with your specific values, and here is where reasonable people disagree."

Prediction and automation are tools in service of deeper human comprehension and better deliberation — not ends in themselves.

We will deliberately design experiences that sometimes *slow people down* and encourage them to read primary sources rather than blindly trusting the twin.

---

## 7. We Are Honest About Current Limitations

Current large language models are impressive but have well-documented problems: biases, inconsistency, hallucination, order sensitivity, and uneven performance across demographic groups.

**We commit to**:
- Never overstating the fidelity of any twin
- Building strong correction and audit interfaces so users can see and fix problems
- Publishing per-group accuracy and bias diagnostics when we have the data
- Treating this as a long-term research program, not a finished product

---

## 8. This Document Can Only Be Changed With Extreme Care

Any change to this Charter requires:
- Clear public justification
- A period for community comment
- A supermajority of active maintainers (once governance exists)

The spirit of this document is more important than the letter. If a future action clearly violates the intent even if it follows the text, it should not happen.

---

## Final Word

Democracy is one of humanity's most important and fragile inventions. Technology can expand its possibilities or accelerate its decay.

Mandate exists because we believe the former is still possible — but only if we move with unusual caution, humility, and respect for the difficulty of the problem.

If you are contributing to this project in any way, you are agreeing to defend these principles even when it is inconvenient, unfashionable, or slows us down.

That is the price of working on something that actually matters.
